What is Subnautica?

Subnautica is an open-world survival game released in 2018 by Unknown Worlds. You play as a crew member of a commercial spaceship that has crash landed on an alien ocean planet, and it is your goal to escape. However, there are a large variety of flora, fauna, and biomes native to the planet, some of which help you and some of which do not. You must scan blueprints, explore new areas, craft vehicles and bases, and conquer your fears to beat the game. With nearly 11 million copies sold on Steam alone, Subnautica is a very popular and well recieved game.

One unique characteristic of Subnautica is the lack of weapons in the game, except for a basic survival knife. This is because the developers of the game wanted to promote non-violence and using peaceful means to win (both as a creative and moral decision).

The Subnautica Collection

Subnautica is just the first game in the series, and there have been two sequels since. Subnautica Below Zero is the first sequel to the game. It is set on the same planet as Subnautica, only in the colder arctic sections (as the name suggests). Though not as populara s the original, it is still a classic among Subnautica fans. The latest addition to the Subnautica collection is Subnautica 2, with the early access releasing in May of this year. Selling approximately 4 million copies in the first week alone, it has been the most successful of the three by far.

Legal Troubles

One of the reasons the release of Subnautica 2 was so delayed was because of the legal troubles between the developers and publisher (Krafton Games). Unknown Worlds sued Krafton in 2025 due to many complex issues, but primarily intentionally delaying the relase of the game in order to not pay a $250 million payout. Unknown Worlds fortunately eventually won and the Subnautica 2 early access was released a couple months ago.
